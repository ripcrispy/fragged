# Code of conduct

Fragged Forums is built by people who like communities. Let's run ours the way we'd want a good forum to be run.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to newcomers. Everyone started somewhere, and "how do I…?" is a fair question.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- Give credit, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the people who'll read them later.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or stalking, in project spaces or aimed at project members elsewhere.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, body, abilities or beliefs.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeatedly ignoring maintainers' requests.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers. Use GitHub's private reporting, or contact a maintainer directly if
you can't. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may respond with anything from a private word, to a warning, to temporary or permanent removal from
project spaces, depending on severity and history. Anyone affected can appeal to a different maintainer.
